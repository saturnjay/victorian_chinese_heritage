Continuous improvement

Help this resource remain useful, careful and open.

Heritage research is never finished. New records emerge, identifications change, links break and communities contribute knowledge that deserves careful review. Donations made through CCCAV help sustain the work behind this portal.

01

Research and verification

Checking place records, biographies, dates, names, coordinates and documentary sources.

02

Digitisation and access

Preparing cemetery records, photographs, maps and archival references for responsible public use.

03

Translation and inclusion

Improving multilingual access, accessibility and representation of diverse community perspectives.

04

Technical stewardship

Maintaining the map, correcting errors, preserving links and improving the portal over time.
